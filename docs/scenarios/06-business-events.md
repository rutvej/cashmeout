# Scenarios: Business & Entrepreneurial Events

This document details supply chain disruptions, staffing conflicts, customer spikes, tax audits, and scaling dilemmas in CashFlow Life Sim.

---

### [BIZ-001] Key Employee Resigns Abruptly
- **Trigger:** Operating a business with ≥ 1 staff member; random (15% annual chance).
- **Prompt:** *"Your lead retail associate / senior developer hands in their 2-day notice for a higher-paying competitor."*
- **Choices:**
  - **A: Match competitor's offer (+$350/mo wage increase)** → Retains staff; operational continuity preserved; operating margin contracts by 4%.
  - **B: Let them walk and manage duties yourself** → Saves wage for 30 days; founder must dedicate **+2 extra daily time slots** to business operations.
  - **C: Spend $500 on an urgent headhunter recruitment agency** → Fills position within 7 days.

---

### [BIZ-002] Supply Chain Price Shock: Wholesale Inflation
- **Trigger:** High-inflation era or commodity price spike; Food Cart or Retail.
- **Prompt:** *"Your primary wholesale packaging and raw ingredients distributor raises unit prices by 22% due to rising fuel costs."*
- **Choices:**
  - **A: Pass 15% price hike to end customers** → Gross revenue stays stable; 10% risk of customer volume drop.
  - **B: Absorb cost increase internally** → Profit margin drops by $400/mo; protects customer loyalty.
  - **C: Spend 2 days vetting alternative discount suppliers** → Incurs temporary operational friction; restores original cost structure.

---

### [BIZ-003] The Viral TikTok / Social Media Surge
- **Trigger:** E-Commerce, Food Cart, or Retail Boutique; random marketing roll.
- **Prompt:** *"A regional influencer reviews your signature dish/product. The video goes viral with 1.2M views! Orders surge 400% overnight."*
- **Choices:**
  - **A: Hustle 14-hour days to fulfill every order** → Founder dedicates max time slots; business clears **+$4,800 windfall profit**; Energy drops to 25%; Physical Health takes -8% hit.
  - **B: Cap orders at existing capacity to protect quality & sanity** → Business clears +$1,500 profit; preserves health and customer reputation.

---

### [BIZ-004] Equipment Catastrophe: Kitchen / Server Meltdown
- **Trigger:** Random operational hazard (10% annual chance).
- **Prompt:** *"Critical Equipment Failure: The commercial refrigeration unit or database server cluster blows a motherboard. Operations are frozen."*
- **Choices:**
  - **A: Emergency same-day replacement ($1,800 cash)** → Resumes operations tomorrow; high one-time outlay.
  - **B: Budget repair service with 5-day lead time ($500)** → Halts business revenue for 5 days (-$800 lost sales).

---

### [BIZ-005] Angel Investor Syndicate Term Sheet
- **Trigger:** E-Commerce or Consulting venture generates ≥ $7,000/mo for 6 consecutive months.
- **Prompt:** *"A boutique angel investor group offers $60,000 in growth capital in exchange for a 25% equity stake in your enterprise."*
- **Choices:**
  - **A: Accept angel funding and dilute equity** → +$60,000 corporate cash buffer; unlocks immediate second-location expansion; founder retains 75% equity.
  - **B: Reject the term sheet and remain 100% bootstrapped** → Retains total ownership and creative autonomy; scales at organic pace.

---

### [BIZ-006] Aggressive Competitor Opens Across the Street
- **Trigger:** Operating Retail or Tutoring Academy; Year 4+.
- **Prompt:** *"A well-funded franchise chain opens 100 yards from your location, launching a predatory 50% discount promotional campaign."*
- **Choices:**
  - **A: Launch counter-promotions and spend $1,000 on local marketing** → Defends customer base; cashflow temporarily depressed for 60 days.
  - **B: Pivot to ultra-premium, personalized customer service** → Slightly lower volume; higher profit margins per client (+5% net profit).

---

### [BIZ-007] Commercial Landlord Lease Renewal Spike
- **Trigger:** Exactly every 36 months of leasing retail/office space.
- **Prompt:** *"Commercial Lease Renewal: The building owner announces a 25% rent increase for the next 3-year term ($2,000/mo → $2,500/mo)."*
- **Choices:**
  - **A: Sign the new lease and stay in the established location** → Monthly OPEX rises by -$500/mo permanently.
  - **B: Relocate shop to a secondary district ($1,600/mo)** → $3,500 moving expense; customer foot traffic drops 15% for first 90 days.

---

### [BIZ-008] Corporate B2B Retainer Opportunity
- **Trigger:** Consulting / Freelance or Tutoring venture.
- **Prompt:** *"A mid-size corporation requests a guaranteed annual B2B retainer: $3,500/month recurring revenue, requiring 15 hours of work per week."*
- **Choices:**
  - **A: Lock in the contract** → Guarantees $3,500/mo predictable revenue; commits 2 daily time slots.
  - **B: Decline to preserve flexibility for high-margin individual projects** → Keeps schedule open.

---

### [BIZ-009] Quarterly Sales Tax Audit
- **Trigger:** Random government audit (8% annual chance).
- **Prompt:** *"Department of Revenue Audit: Auditors request all sales receipts and business deductions for the past two fiscal years."*
- **Choices:**
  - **A: Hire an accredited CPA to represent you ($800)** → Clean audit; zero fines; complete peace of mind.
  - **B: Represent yourself with bookkeeping spreadsheets** → Saves $800; burns 3 days of time; 40% chance of minor bookkeeper penalty ($600).

---

### [BIZ-010] Voluntary Business Sale: The Exit Offer
- **Trigger:** Venture has run profitably for ≥ 3 years with annual profit > $30,000.
- **Prompt:** *"An industry aggregator offers to acquire your business outright for a 3.5x EBITDA multiple ($115,000 cash buyout)."*
- **Choices:**
  - **A: Accept the acquisition and exit** → Receives $115,000 lump sum (subject to LTCG tax); business closes; frees all daily time slots.
  - **B: Retain the business and keep compounding cashflow** → Keeps generating monthly profits.
