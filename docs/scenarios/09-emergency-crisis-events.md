# Scenarios: Emergency, Crisis & Debt Spiral Events

This document details acute financial shocks, medical emergencies, catastrophic equipment losses, and the anatomy of the multi-stage debt spiral in CashFlow Life Sim.

---

### [EMG-001] The Uninsured Emergency Room Catastrophe
- **Severity:** Catastrophic.
- **Trigger:** Health insurance tier is "None" (Uninsured), acute medical event triggers.
- **Emergency Fund Needed:** $7,500+.
- **Prompt:** *"Acute Cholecystitis: You are rushed to the regional hospital requiring an emergency gallbladder removal. The final itemized billing statement totals $8,200."*
- **Choices:**
  - **A: If Emergency Fund ≥ $8,200:** Pay cash in full → Liquid reserves depleted, but balance sheet survives with **$0 in debt and unblemished credit score**.
  - **B: If Emergency Fund < $8,200:** Enroll in Hospital Medical Debt Financing ($8,200 @ 18% APR) → Adds a crushing **$450/mo EMI for 24 months**; net cashflow pushed into deficit.
  - **C: Default on the hospital bill:** Credit score plunges -120 points; account forwarded to aggressive third-party collection agencies.

### [EMG-002] The Sudden Corporate Layoff & Jobless Squeeze
- **Severity:** Major.
- **Trigger:** Macroeconomic recession or corporate bankruptcy.
- **Emergency Fund Needed:** 3 to 6 months of living expenses.
- **Prompt:** *"Your firm shuts its doors with zero severance warning. Your $4,000/mo salary vanishes immediately. Your monthly living expenses are $2,200."*
- **Scenarios by Buffer Level:**
  - **3+ Months Buffer ($6,600+):** Player remains calm, uses daily slots to upskill, attends interviews without desperation, and lands an equivalent or superior job within 60 days.
  - **0 Months Buffer ($0):** Immediate panic. Inability to pay rent triggers landlord eviction notices. Player is forced to accept a minimum-wage manual delivery role ($1,500/mo), locking them into an exhausting poverty trap.

---

### [EMG-003] Major Automotive Collision & Total Loss
- **Severity:** Major.
- **Trigger:** Player commutes via personal vehicle; random collision event (5% annual chance).
- **Prompt:** *"A distracted driver runs a red light and t-bones your vehicle. The chassis is totaled."*
- **Choices:**
  - **A: If Vehicle Insurance Active:** Insurer pays market replacement value ($14,000); player pays $500 deductible; buys replacement car cleanly.
  - **B: If Vehicle Uninsured:** Total write-off. Remaining auto loan ($8,000) must still be paid off out-of-pocket with no vehicle to show for it.

---

### [EMG-004] The Commercial Tenant Bankruptcy & Void
- **Trigger:** Player owns commercial or residential rental property.
- **Prompt:** *"Your investment flat tenant declares personal bankruptcy, leaves in the middle of the night, and leaves behind significant drywall damage ($2,500 repairs required)."*
- **Choices:**
  - **A: Absorb repairs from emergency reserve and re-list ($2,500 cash)** → Restores property within 30 days.
  - **B: Leave property shuttered due to lack of repair capital** → Property generates $0 income while mortgage EMI ($450/mo) continues draining personal cash every month.

### [EMG-005] The Multi-Stage Debt Spiral
- **Severity:** Catastrophic / Existential.
- **Trigger:** Emergency fund is $0 AND player suffers two minor shocks in consecutive months.

#### Phase 1: The Initial Fracture
- Furnace breaks ($1,200). Player has no cash, so charges it to a credit card at 26% APR.

#### Phase 2: The Squeeze
- The new minimum credit card payment ($90/mo) consumes the thin margin of monthly savings.

#### Phase 3: The Secondary Shock
- Next month, car tires blow out ($450). Card is maxed out. Player accepts an unsecured payday advance ($500 with a $150 finance fee).

#### Phase 4: Insolvency Collapse
- Bank auto-debit for rent bounces. Credit score plummets below 520. Bank refuses any consolidation refinancing.
- Player enters the **"Survival Mode"** game loop: forced overtime, canceling gym/nutrition, and selling personal assets at pawnshop prices to avoid eviction.

---

### [EMG-006] Recovery Paths from Bankruptcy / Distress
1. **The Debt Avalanche Strategy:** Freeze all discretionary spending envelopes to $0 on Salary Day; direct every surplus penny to the highest APR balance.
2. **Asset Liquidation:** Sell equities or crypto, even at a paper loss, to eliminate toxic 20%+ consumer interest.
3. **Credit Rehabilitation:** 12 consecutive months of zero missed payments gradually restores credit score from 520 back to 680+.
