# 10 — Life Goals & Year-10 Life Report

CashFlow Life Sim is not a game of arbitrary points or fixed victory flags. It evaluates your life at the conclusion of a 10-year journey against the personal targets you set on Day 1.

---

## 1. Selecting Life Goals (Onboarding Wizard)

During initial character setup (Age 22), the player selects **one primary ambition from each of three life domains**:

### Category A: Financial & Wealth Ambition
1. **The Safety Fortress:** Accumulate at least **$50,000 in liquid, debt-free cash/savings**.
2. **The Six-Figure Net Worth:** Achieve a total verifiable **Net Worth of $100,000+**.
3. **The Quarter-Million Tycoon:** Attain a net worth exceeding **$250,000** through business equity, stocks, or real estate.
4. **Complete Debt Elimination:** Own all assets with **$0 in total outstanding debt/loans**.
5. **Passive Freedom:** Generate **$1,500+/month in non-labor passive income** (dividends + rental profit).

### Category B: Health & Vitality Ambition
1. **Olympic Resilience:** Maintain average Physical Health **above 85%** across the decade.
2. **Burnout Inoculation:** Never trigger a single clinical burnout, mental collapse, or forced stress leave.
3. **Iron Consistency:** Maintain an average gym attendance of **at least 3.5 sessions per week** across 10 years.
4. **Youthful Vitality:** Conclude Year 10 with Energy levels **above 80%**.

### Category C: Career & Lifestyle Ambition
1. **Homeowner Pride:** Own a primary residential home with positive equity.
2. **Thriving Entrepreneur:** Build and operate a venture producing **$4,000+/month in net profits**.
3. **Executive Leader:** Reach Director or VP rank in corporate hierarchy.
4. **The Balanced Life:** Maintain a decade-long average Mental Health and Happiness score of **75%+**.

---

## 2. In-Game Goal Tracker HUD

A discreet progress meter sits within the top navigation bar throughout the game:
- Displays percentage completion for each of the 3 chosen goals (e.g., *Wealth: 64% | Vitality: 82% | Lifestyle: 100%*).
- Tapping the widget reveals remaining milestones and estimated time to completion based on current monthly trajectory.

### Grade Criteria
- **Grade A (90–100%):** 3/3 goals achieved, positive net worth, no debt defaults, health > 75%.
- **Grade B (75–89%):** 2/3 goals achieved, solvent balance sheet, minor health/stress scars.
- **Grade C (60–74%):** 1/3 goals achieved, high debt service burden, significant health degradation.
- **Grade D (45–59%):** 0 goals achieved, underwater net worth or chronic debt distress.
- **Grade F (< 45%):** Bankruptcy, insolvency, or prolonged medical collapse.
