## 1. Insurance System

Insurance serves as the ultimate shield against catastrophic balance-sheet wipeouts.

### A. Health Insurance Tiers

| Tier | Monthly Premium | Inpatient / Emergency Coverage | Deductible Out-of-Pocket | Max Annual Out-of-Pocket |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **None (Uninsured)** | $0 / mo | 0% covered | 100% of bill paid in cash | Unlimited (Risk of complete bankruptcy) |
| **Basic Bronze** | $50 / mo | 50% covered | $500 deductible | $5,000 max |
| **Standard Silver** | $120 / mo | 80% covered | $250 deductible | $2,500 max |
| **Premium Gold** | $250 / mo | 95% covered | $0 deductible | $1,000 max |

#### Real Consequence Comparison: Acute Appendectomy ($8,400 Bill)
- **Uninsured Player:** Pays full $8,400 out-of-pocket. If cash balance is under $8,400, triggers a forced 22% interest medical loan and tanks credit score.
- **Basic Tier:** Pays $500 deductible + 50% of remaining $7,900 = **$4,450**.
- **Standard Tier:** Pays $250 deductible + 20% of remaining $8,150 = **$1,880**.
- **Premium Tier:** Pays 5% = **$420**.

### B. Other Insurance Policies
1. **Vehicle Insurance ($40–$75/mo):** Mandatory if car/scooter is owned. Uninsured collision costs $3,000–$6,000 in repairs plus third-party liabilities.
2. **Term Life Insurance ($35–$90/mo):** Unlocks upon marriage or childbirth. If player experiences critical illness or incapacity, provides a $250,000 safety cushion to dependents.
3. **Homeowners / Property Insurance ($45–$110/mo):** Covers structural and fire catastrophes on owned real estate.

## 2. Progressive Income Taxation

Taxes are evaluated annually during the Year-End Financial Audit (Month 12 / Day 360).

### Annual Individual Income Tax Brackets

| Annual Taxable Income Range | Marginal Tax Rate | Example Tax on $45,000 Income |
|---|---|---|
| **$0 to $15,000** | 0% | $0 |
| **$15,001 to $35,000** | 10% | $2,000 (10% of $20,000) |
| **$35,001 to $65,000** | 20% | $2,000 (20% of remaining $10,000) |
| **$65,001 and above** | 30% | — |
| **Total Tax Liability on $45,000:** | | **$4,000** |

### Tax Provisioning & Penalties
On Salary Day, players can opt to auto-reserve 10–25% of their paycheck into a dedicated "Tax Provision Escrow." 
- If the escrow covers the year-end bill, taxes clear seamlessly.
- If underfunded and liquid cash is inadequate, the tax agency levies a **15% statutory penalty** and auto-debits remaining accounts into overdraft.

## 3. Credit Score Engine (Range: 350 to 850)

Your credit score governs loan approval likelihood and interest rate premiums.

| Credit Score Tier | Label | Unsecured Personal Loan APR | 25-Year Mortgage APR |
|---|---|---|---|
| **760 to 850** | Prime / Excellent | 8.5% | 4.8% |
| **680 to 759** | Good | 11.5% | 5.5% |
| **600 to 679** | Fair / Subprime | 17.0% | 7.2% |
| **350 to 599** | Severely Impaired | 26.0% (Or Rejected) | Automatic Mortgage Denial |

### Score Modifiers
- **On-time EMI payment:** +3 points/month.
- **Missed EMI payment:** -45 points per occurrence.
- **Settling / Defaulting on a loan:** -120 points.
- **Credit card utilization > 70%:** -15 points/month.
- **Full loan payoff celebration:** +25 points.
