# 01 — Game Overview

## Concept

**CashFlow Life Sim** is a 10-year life simulation game (10–20 minute playthrough).

The player starts at age 22 and simulates financial and life decisions month by month for 10 years. Every decision has consequences that compound over time. There are no right answers — only trade-offs.

> *"Fast-forward your life. Make real-world decisions. See the consequences. Learn without real-world cost."*

---

## Core Loop

```
DAY 1 (Salary Day)
  → Income arrives
  → Auto-debits run
  → Player sets monthly plan (savings, investments, budget)
  → Player sets daily schedule defaults for the month

DAYS 2–28 (Daily Life)
  → Random events fire (career, health, financial, life)
  → Player resolves event cards (2–4 choices each)
  → Player can manually: buy/sell stocks, apply for jobs, manage business
  → Daily defaults auto-apply (gym, commute, sleep)
  → Time conflicts resolved as they happen

YEAR-END (Annual Review)
  → Income tax calculated and shown
  → Performance appraisal
  → Business annual tax filing
  → Property appreciation update
  → Health annual checkup trend
  → Inflation summary

YEAR 10 — LIFE REPORT
  → Goals vs. reality comparison
  → Key life moments timeline
  → Financial snapshot
  → Letter grade + reflection
```

---

## The Four Pillars

All four run simultaneously. All compete for the same pool of **Time · Health · Money**.

| Pillar | What it covers |
|--------|---------------|
| 💼 Career | Job ladder, applications, company culture, layoffs, courses |
| 📈 Investments | Stocks, MF SIP, FD, crypto, dividends |
| 🏪 Business | Own venture, employees, marketing, expansion, business loans |
| 🏠 Property | Personal home, investment property, rental income |

---

## The Three Resources

Everything in the game is a trade-off between these three:

| Resource | Measured by | Depletes from | Refills from |
|----------|-------------|--------------|-------------|
| ⏰ Time | 24hrs/day | Work, commute, events | Cannot refill — lost forever |
| 🏥 Health | 3 bars (0–100) | Stress, bad food, no gym, poor sleep | Gym, food, sleep, vacation |
| 💰 Money | Cash balance | Expenses, EMI, taxes, emergencies | Salary, business, investments |

---

## The 10-Year Timeline

| Year | Life Stage | New Mechanics |
|------|-----------|---------------|
| 1–2 | Early career | Basic job events, salary day, behavioral loop |
| 3–4 | First inflection | Courses unlock, first job switch, SIP available |
| 4–5 | Mid-career | Market correction, layoff risk, side hustle option |
| 5–6 | Major decisions | Home buying, marriage possible, business option |
| 6–7 | Wealth building | Investment property, business scaling |
| 7–8 | Consequences | Burnout risk, health visible, debt spiral or freedom |
| 8–9 | Senior career | Director/VP roles, high salary + high stress |
| 10 | Life Report | Goals vs. reality, final snapshot |

---

## The Behavioral Heart

Bad decisions compound into more bad events. Good habits protect against disruption.

- Say yes to impulse buying → more temptation events next month
- Skip gym repeatedly → motivation collapse → health declines faster
- Sleep < 6hrs → energy crash → work performance drops → stress rises
- **Counters never reach 0** — there is always temptation. Life is always uncertain.

---

## Life Goals

Set at onboarding. One per category. Compared against at Year 10.

| Category | Examples |
|----------|---------|
| 💰 Money | $50k saved / $100k net worth / debt-free / $200k wealth |
| 🏥 Health | Stay above 70% / Never burn out / Gym avg 3x/week |
| ⏰ Life | Own your home / $2k/month side income / Work-life balance |

---

## Game End — Life Report

At Year 10 the game ends. No win, no lose. A **Life Report** is shown:
- Goals set vs. goals achieved
- Key life moments timeline
- Financial snapshot (cash, portfolio, debt, net worth)
- Health at end (physical, mental, energy)
- Credit score
- Overall letter grade (A–F based on goal achievement)

Player can then restart with a new onboarding.

## Build Phases

### Phase 1 — Core Job Life (Fully Playable)
- Onboarding (name, starting job, 3 life goals)
- Salary Day card (monthly financial evaluation)
- Daily schedule defaults (gym, sleep, commute)
- Job board — apply, get rejected, get offers
- Company profiles with culture ratings
- Career ladder with course/experience requirements
- Courses → resume → unlocks jobs
- Layoffs / getting fired
- Annual appraisal
- Behavioral feedback loop
- Basic stock buy/sell
- Crypto asset (high risk)
- Insurance system (health, vehicle)
- Emergency fund mechanic + spiral event
- Year-end tax
- NPC FOMO events
- Vacation / mental health recovery
- Life Report at Year 10

### Phase 2 — Full Investments & Property
- MF SIP system
- FD open/close/maturity
- Dividend auto-credit events
- Personal home buying + mortgage
- Investment property + rental income
- Market cycles (bull/bear/recession)
- Credit score system
- Inflation annual events
- Lifestyle creep mechanic

### Phase 3 — Business Branch
- Business type selection + startup flow
- Monthly business review (salary day extension)
- Business events (supplier, staff, tax, orders)
- Business loans
- Employee management (hire, fire)
- Business to job transition (and back)

### Phase 4 — Relationships & Polish
- Dating → marriage → kids flow
- Partner income / joint finances
- Children cost and time impact
- Parents needing help events
- Full NPC comparison (8 NPCs making different choices)
- Education loan option for courses
- Tutorial (first-time player guidance)
- More event variety per sector
- Shareable game seed
